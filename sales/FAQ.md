# Sales FAQ

### Does it automatically apply?
No. It prepares a ranked application queue and proposal drafts. Submission remains human-confirmed.

### Does it guarantee work?
No. Results depend on the freelancer, job market, profile, proposal quality, and client decisions.

### Can I use my own profile?
Yes. The product is configurable around verified profile facts.

### Can it invent experience?
No. Unsupported claims are intentionally blocked by the proposal layer.

### Does it spend Connects automatically?
No. Connects are checked before submission and final submission requires explicit confirmation.

### Is it affiliated with Upwork?
No. It is independent third-party software. Users remain responsible for platform compliance.
