# Buyer FAQ

### Does it apply for jobs or spend Connects?
No. It exports editable drafts. You submit manually on Upwork.

### Is this an AI model or an earnings system?
The offline edition uses templates and configurable scoring. It does not call an AI model or guarantee work or revenue.

### Does it need an Upwork API key?
The offline export workflow does not. Optional live job synchronization needs approved developer access from Upwork. The seller cannot guarantee approval or supply someone else's account.

### Does it connect Fiverr or Bionluk messages?
No. There is no message API. Bionluk and Payhip orders are tracked manually or imported by CSV.

### What is included in the source package?
Code, configuration example, fictional job data, tests and setup/operating documentation. Credentials, the seller's private profile, actual job exports and customer records are excluded.

### Can it verify my qualifications or Connects?
It compares supplied facts and exported values. You must check them against your current profile and current job before submitting.

### What are the running costs?
The offline assistant has no paid API dependency. Hosted deployments require the buyer's database, hosting and optional provider services; their costs and authorization are separate.
